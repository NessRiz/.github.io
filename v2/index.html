Who we work with

Three kinds of organisations find their way to Aurien.

They come from different markets and different stages. What they share is the need to change their operating model to grow.

  • 01 Australian technology companies preparing to enter the US market through channel and distribution models. The partner infrastructure needed to scale in a new market is easy to underestimate, and getting it wrong early is expensive. Aurien works through the implications before the commitment is made.
  • 02 Australian growth-stage businesses that have scaled faster than their operating model can support. Aurien helps founders and leadership teams work out where the model has broken down and rebuild it for the size the business has become.
  • 03 US-based technology companies whose channel and partner programmes are underperforming. Aurien works embedded alongside specialist channel consultancies to find why a programme isn't producing and fix it from the inside.
02
How this works

Most consultants arrive with frameworks. We arrive with questions.

Every engagement starts with Clarity: a structured diagnostic, not a discovery call in disguise. The problem that prompts the call is rarely the constraint underneath it. Businesses that outgrow their operating model all look different on the surface. Underneath, it's usually the same three or four patterns.

We've built and run these systems inside businesses we were accountable for. Nothing gets recommended until the specific operating context is understood.

Operational pattern recognition, built over years inside growing businesses.

03
AI & operational foundations

AI is only as useful as the operations underneath it.

Aurien has spent the last two years building AI-assisted workflows into live business operations, and runs them every day.

AI adoption keeps running into the same problem: adding intelligence to a system that doesn't yet have reliable foundations.

  • The process is manual and undocumented.
  • The data is inconsistent.
  • No one has a shared view of what good looks like.

AI doesn't fix any of that. It amplifies it.

The advisory work comes from that experience, not from reading about it.

04
The engagement

How an engagement works.

Clarity

Fixed scope and fixed fee

Four to six weeks, run as a structured diagnostic. The output is a written view of where the model is breaking and why, talked through with the leadership team: what to fix first, and what can wait.

Build

Scoped after Clarity

The deeper work. Operating model design, process redesign, channel and market-entry foundations, strategy-to-execution alignment, done alongside the leadership team.

Grow

Advisory and subscription-based

An ongoing fractional engagement for businesses that want an experienced operational and channel strategy hand available without a full-time hire. Scoped to the specific need.

Aurien's channel and partner work covers programme design, partner onboarding infrastructure, go-to-market alignment across markets, and the operational systems that keep a programme running consistently rather than in bursts.
